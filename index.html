about

I've been turning as a hobby for twenty years — my first lathe was a barmitzvah gift from my grandfather. I now work in the micro-studio I designed and built in my garden in Tottenham. My favourite pieces are simple, tactile, and built around imperfections in the wood: burrs, bark, water stains. I offer commissions for gifts and special occasions.

work
commissions

I take commissions for gifts such as ring boxes, kitchenware, and decorative pieces.

I specialise in judaica — mezuzot, seder plates, etrog and kiddush cup boxes, etz chaim for sifrei torah, and so on. I'm one of very few woodturners outside Israel making these on a bespoke basis.

Commissions start at £150 with a lead time of 4 weeks (if you've left it late for a gift I might be able to expedite. Ask!). Fill out the form below and I'll be in touch to begin the design process together.
